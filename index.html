Deniz Drive Assistant

A private, single-user automation tool that connects to its owner's own Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides, and Google Calendar accounts through the official Google APIs.

What it does

  • Reads, creates, and organizes files in the owner's Google Drive
  • Creates and updates Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides documents
  • Reads and manages events in the owner's Google Calendars
  • Manages the owner's own Google Apps Script projects

Who uses it

This is a personal tool operated by its owner for personal use only. It is not offered as a public service, it has no other end users, and it is not distributed to third parties.

Data & privacy
