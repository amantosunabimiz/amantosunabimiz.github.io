Önmuhasebe app

Önmuhasebe app is a private tool for personal expense and pre-accounting tracking. It works with the owner's own Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides, Google Calendar and Google Apps Script accounts through the official Google APIs.

What Önmuhasebe app does

  • Keeps the owner's expense records and category data in Google Sheets
  • Reads, creates, and organizes files in the owner's Google Drive
  • Creates and updates Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides documents
  • Reads and manages events in the owner's Google Calendars
  • Manages the owner's own Google Apps Script projects

Who uses it

Önmuhasebe app is operated by its owner for personal use only. It is not offered as a public service, it has no other end users, and it is not distributed to third parties.
