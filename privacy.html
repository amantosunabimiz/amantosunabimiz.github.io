Privacy Policy

Deniz Drive Assistant · Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy describes how Deniz Drive Assistant ("the tool") accesses and handles information obtained through Google APIs. The tool is a private, single-user application operated by its owner for personal use. It has no other end users.

1. Information the tool accesses

After you grant consent, the tool may access the following, strictly on your own Google Account:

  • Google Drive files and metadata – file names, identifiers, folder structure, and the contents of documents you choose to work with.
  • Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides content – the text, cells, and slides of documents the tool reads or edits on your instruction.
  • Google Calendar data – calendars, events, and their details.
  • Google Apps Script projects – the script projects and deployment settings of the account owner.
  • Basic account information – your email address and display name, used only to identify the connected account.

The tool does not access Gmail, Contacts, Google Photos, or any Google service other than those listed above.

2. How the information is used

Accessed data is used exclusively to perform the actions you request (for example, reading a spreadsheet, creating a document, or adding a calendar event). It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose unrelated to your direct instructions.

3. Storage and security

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's own device in permission-restricted files. They are not transmitted to or stored on any third-party server.
  • The tool runs locally. There is no hosted backend that receives your Google data.
  • Document contents are processed in memory only for the duration of the requested operation and are not retained in any separate database or archive.

4. Sharing and disclosure

No Google user data is sold, rented, transferred, or disclosed to any third party. No data is used for training machine learning models, and no human reads your data except you, the account owner.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Deniz Drive Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

The tool does not retain copies of your Google data after an operation completes. You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately invalidates the stored tokens and the tool can no longer reach your data. To request deletion of any locally stored token data, contact the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
